Physical model notes (approximate, consistent with published descriptions):
• Both configurations use a single-wire / linear monopole driven by modulated current. Classical transverse TEM fields form concentrically around the wire body; the longitudinal / potential component is strongest at the open tip where the return path is cancelled (balun or open end).
• Nikolova–Zimmerman: |A|(r) ≈ (μ₀ · I · Leff)/(4π r) · e−αr   (Leff = 0.15 m). Units: V·s/m (Wb/m).
• Hively: |Er|(r) ≈ (η₀ · I · κ)/(2π r) · e−αr   (κ ≈ 0.4 geometric factor for short gradient-driven monopole). Units: V/m. Derived from spherical-wave solution under More-Complete Electrodynamics (MCE) with B = 0.
• Media attenuation α is deliberately reduced relative to classical skin-effect values, reflecting the claimed SLW property of penetrating conductive media with far lower loss (see Hively US 9,306,527 and Zimmerman QEX 2011). Space uses pure geometric 1/r spreading.
• Charts show amplitude vs distance on log–log axes out to 500 km. Values are order-of-magnitude estimates for educational / exploratory simulation; actual experimental calibration requires measured antenna factors and detector responsivity.
